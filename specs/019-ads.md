# S019: Ads (AdMob)

State: Implemented; real ads need the user's AdMob account; device check pending.
Source: user request (2026-10-10): monetize in stages, ads first (RevenueCat and paywalls later);
"getting more money by watching ads, but not very noticeable money". Related decision: D014.

## Problem

The game has no income. Ads come first; they must not sell power or break the money challenge.

## Scope and exclusions

- Rewarded ad, sponsor bonus: during a transfer window, watch an ad for +1% of the season's
  wage bill (at least $10K), so it scales with the club. No limit per window (user's choice,
  2026-10-10; was 2% once per window). Shown as one line on the Transfers tab.
- Rewarded ad, free scouting: a market player's sheet offers "Free with ad" next to paid scouting.
- Interstitial: when starting the next season, at most once every 10 minutes, never in the
  first season or the Daily Challenge.
- Consent: Google's UMP consent form (EU) before ads load; iOS tracking text in the app config.
- Where native ads are unavailable (Expo Go, web) a development-only simulated ad stands in,
  so the flow can be tried; release builds without native ads show no ad options.
- Test ad units in development and until real AdMob IDs are configured.
- Excluded: banners, ads during matches or transfers, paid "remove ads" (comes with RevenueCat).
